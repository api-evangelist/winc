Sometimes a glass of wine is a way to mentally clock out of your inbox and into somewhere sunnier, slower, or just more interesting than your living room. You don’t need a boarding pass to experience that feeling. This guide is about choosing bottles that make you feel like you’re in your dream sipping destination, whether that’s a European escape or a Golden State getaway.
