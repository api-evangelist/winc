National Rosé Day (June 14, 2025) is around the corner—and we’re celebrating in style. Whether you’re poolside, beach-bound, or hosting friends, these easy, refreshing rosé cocktail recipes are your go-to. From a fizzy Rosé Lemonade to frozen Strawberry Frosé and a zesty Summer on the Rocks, there’s a sip for every summer vibe.
